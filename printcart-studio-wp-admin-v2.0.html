Help & Quick start

New here? This explains how Printcart Studio works and links you straight to each step.

Get your first gallery live in 4 steps
1
Create a job
Set up one shoot for a school or club and pick its studio type (ICP).
2
Import your roster
Upload the student list (CSV/Excel). We match the columns for you and link siblings.
3
Capture & upload
Shoot with QR cards (or AI face matching), then drop the whole batch in. We crop, retouch and match automatically.
4
Publish
Send each parent a private, password-free link to buy. Track opens & orders in Email.
Key concepts
Agency → Job → Subject → AlbumAgency = the school/club. Job = one shoot. Subject = one person. Album = that person’s photos.
Go to Jobs →
QR vs AI matchingQR cards = photograph a printed code before each person (most accurate). AI matching (PRO) = compare faces to reference photos, no cards.
Set up QR cards →
TokensYou spend about 1 token per photo processed. Buy packs or subscribe in Billing & Pricing.
Open Billing →
Magic-link portalParents open a one-tap email link — no signup. Around 80% buy on their phone.
Preview portal →
Family bundlesSiblings are detected automatically so parents can buy all their kids together at a discount.
See Clients →
Confidence badgesGreen = AI is sure, amber = double-check, red = likely wrong. Review amber & red first.
Open Review queue →
Handy tips
  • ✓In the Review queue, press Y / N / R to confirm, reject or reassign fast.
  • ✓Big uploads keep running after you leave — we email you when they’re done.
  • ✓Premium (PRO) unlocks AI matching, composites, 9-up QR and custom branding.
Still stuck?

Email support@printcart.com or open the in-app chat — we usually reply within a few hours.
